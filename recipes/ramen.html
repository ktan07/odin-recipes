Description

This recipe for vegan Curry Ramen is my current favorite bowl of soup. It takes only a few minutes to prepare and it’s also mostly pantry ingredients with a few fresh ingredients thrown in so it’s pretty inexpensive. Plus, it’s a great way to get extra veggies into your diet while also enjoying delicious comfort food.

Ingredients

  • 2 packages ramen
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 8 ounces baby bella mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 cups broccoli, cut into florets
  • 1 cup full fat coconut milk
  • pepper to taste
  • optional: Green onions, cilantro, limes, toasted sesame seeds, sriracha

Steps

  • Cook ramen according to package instructions, omitting the seasoning packet. Drain and divide into 2 serving bowls.
  • In a separate pot, heat sesame oil over medium heat. Add mushrooms and cook, stirring frequently, until softened.
  • Add garlic, ginger, and curry paste. Cook, stirring the entire time, until fragrant, only 30 seconds or so.
  • Add peanut butter, soy sauce, and vegetable broth. Bring to a boil.
  • Add broccoli; cook until broccoli is tender, 4 - 5 minutes.
  • Stir in coconut milk.
  • Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  • Ladle broth and vegetables over the cooked ramen noodles.
  • Garnish with green onions, cilantro, limes, sesame seeds, and sriracha, if desired.
